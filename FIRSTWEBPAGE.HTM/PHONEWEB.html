Samsung Galaxy s26 Ultra

Samsung’s flagship phone has never felt quite so at odds with itself. There’s no magnetic Qi2 charging, which puts it a touch behind the Pixel 10 and iPhone 17 ranges, and it’s now more expensive than before, making it even more of an investment for true Samsung diehards. However, even with these setbacks, the Galaxy S26 Ultra is still one of the best Android phones around and a true joy to use..

price:#850.000
